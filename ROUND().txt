ROUND() in Oracle SQL is a math function used to round a number to a specified number of decimal places or to round dates to a specific unit (like day, month, or year).

1. Numeric ROUND()
********************

Syntax
======
ROUND(number, [ decimal_places ])

number: The numeric value or column to round.

decimal_places: (Optional) The number of decimal places to round to:

Positive number: Rounds to that many places to the right of the decimal point.

Zero (0) or omitted: Rounds to the nearest integer.

Negative number: Rounds to that many places to the left of the decimal point (e.g., tens, hundreds).


Examples
========
SELECT 
    ROUND(125.786, 2)  AS round_two,     -- 125.79
    ROUND(125.786, 0)  AS round_zero,    -- 126
    ROUND(125.786)     AS round_default, -- 126 (omitted defaults to 0)
    ROUND(125.786, -1) AS round_tens,    -- 130
    ROUND(125.786, -2) AS round_hundreds -- 100
FROM dual;



2. Date ROUND()
*****************

Oracle also allows you to round date values to the nearest unit of time (e.g., midnight, month start, or year start).

Syntax
======
ROUND(date, [ format_unit ])

SELECT 
    -- Rounds to the nearest midnight
    ROUND(TO_DATE('2026-09-06 15:30:00', 'YYYY-MM-DD HH24:MI:SS')) AS round_day, 
    -- Result: 2026-09-07 00:00:00 (past 12 PM rolls to next day)

    -- Rounds to the start of the nearest month
    ROUND(TO_DATE('2026-09-20', 'YYYY-MM-DD'), 'MM') AS round_month,
    -- Result: 2026-10-01 (past the 15th rolls to next month)

    -- Rounds to the start of the nearest year
    ROUND(TO_DATE('2026-04-10', 'YYYY-MM-DD'), 'YEAR') AS round_year
    -- Result: 2026-01-01 (before July 1 rolls to current year start)
FROM dual;
