/*

1965. Employees With Missing Information

1. Understand the problem
You are given two tables:

Employees
employee_id	name
2	Crew
4	Haven
5	Kristian

Salaries
employee_id	salary
5	76071
1	22517
4	63539

You need to find employees whose information is missing from either table.

In other words:
1. Employee exists in Employees but not in Salaries.
2. Employee exists in Salaries but not in Employees.
Expected output:
employee_id
1
2


*/


SELECT employee_id
FROM Employees
WHERE employee_id NOT IN (
    SELECT employee_id FROM Salaries
)

UNION

SELECT employee_id
FROM Salaries
WHERE employee_id NOT IN (
    SELECT employee_id FROM Employees
)

ORDER BY employee_id;
